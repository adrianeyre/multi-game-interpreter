# Passport to Adventure

SCUMM v4 — demo compilation

**No Wikipedia article covers this title.** There is nothing to transcribe and
nothing to cite, so this README carries only what `docs/released-games.md` and
this project's own notes say — which is less than its neighbours have, and is
left short rather than padded out to match them (ADR 0031).

_No box art here: no article to take one from, and nothing is substituted from
elsewhere._

What it is, from `docs/released-games.md`: a **demo compilation** LucasArts
shipped carrying playable slices of three SCUMM v4 games — _Indiana Jones and
the Last Crusade_, _Loom_ and _The Secret of Monkey Island_. One package, one
Version, three games' worth of data, which is why it is listed as a title of
its own rather than folded into any of the three.

## How it runs here

**SCUMM v4 has a real game behind it.** Loom's CD release has been played to
the distaff here and its drafts play. This title is a different install of the
same Version, so it is worth trying rather than relied upon, and
**Completable** (`CONTEXT.md`) is claimed for no title.

## Where it sits in this project

`docs/released-games.md` lists this title under **SCUMM v4 — demo
compilation**. That page is the scope statement for the whole catalogue and
says, per Engine family and per Version, what has actually been run rather than
what is implemented — **Completable** (`CONTEXT.md`) is claimed for no title on
it.
