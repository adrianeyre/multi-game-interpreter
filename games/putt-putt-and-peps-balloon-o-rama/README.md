# Putt-Putt & Pep's Balloon-o-Rama

HE 95

![Box art: Putt-Putt and Pep](image.jpg)

Putt-Putt is a series of children's adventure and puzzle computer games created
by Humongous Entertainment. This franchise was Humongous Entertainment's first
game series to be developed. They primarily involve clicking to get to a
destination, although some sub-quests and mini-games involve the keyboard. The
main character, Putt-Putt, an anthropomorphic purple convertible, and his dog,
Pep, travel to various locations.

**Putt-Putt (series)**, which covers it, and the link under **Elsewhere** goes
there rather than to a page about this game alone._

## How it runs here

**Humongous titles are forks of SCUMM v6**, versioned separately as HE 60
through HE 100. v6 itself runs, draws and saves here; the HE extensions on top
of it are not separately verified, and the exact game-to-HE-number mapping in
`docs/released-games.md` is explicitly approximate. Worth trying, not relied
upon.

## Where it sits in this project

`docs/released-games.md` lists this title under **HE 95**. That page is the

Version, what has actually been run rather than what is implemented —
